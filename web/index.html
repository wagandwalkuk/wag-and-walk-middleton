Who It Is For

A proper online home for small dog businesses

Wag & Web is for pet-care businesses that need something better than a half-finished social profile, but do not want corporate agency waffle or a huge project.

It is made for people who are busy walking dogs, grooming coats, visiting cats, training puppies or building a local client base and need a website that explains the service clearly.

Why Wag & Walk is offering this

Wag & Walk has had to build the same foundations every local dog business needs: a clear offer, local search pages, contact routes, reviews, practical policies and a launch checklist that does not get in the way of the real work.

Wag & Web FAQs

Who is Wag & Web for?

Dog walkers, groomers, pet sitters, trainers and small pet-care businesses that need a clear website and local online presence.
