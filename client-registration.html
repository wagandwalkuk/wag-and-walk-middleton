Client Registration

Thanks for choosing Wag & Walk Middleton. Before your dog’s first walk, please complete the short registration form below so we have the details needed to keep walks safe, smooth and reliable.

Simple & Reassuring

What the form covers

The form brings the practical details from your meet and greet into one clear place before care begins.

  • Owner contact and emergency details
  • Your dog’s details and medical information
  • Behaviour, walking notes and preferred time window
  • Vet authorisation and emergency arrangements
  • Photo and social media permission
  • Confirmation that you have read the service policies

When should I complete it?

After your meet and greet and before your dog’s first walk. Wag & Walk Middleton will send you the form when it is time to register.

Information We Will Ask For

What to have ready

Owner & emergency details

Name, address, telephone, email and an emergency contact.

Dog & veterinary details

Name, breed, age, sex, neuter status, vet practice and contact number.

Walking & access

Duration, time window, typical days, access instructions, equipment, recall and training cues.

Behaviour & safety

Biting, snapping, dog fights, chasing, escaping, reactivity, traffic worries and lead behaviour.

Medical information

Conditions, medication, allergies, mobility issues and heat sensitivity.

Permissions

Emergency vet authorisation, responsibility for veterinary costs, media choices, policy acceptance and electronic signature.
