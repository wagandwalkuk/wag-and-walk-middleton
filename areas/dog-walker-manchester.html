Services available for Manchester clients

Subject to availability and travel time, services may include 30 minute solo dog walks, 60 minute solo dog walks, pet visits and pick-up and drop-off support. A 30 minute walk is ideal for a toilet break, a shorter exercise session or a calm reset during the day. Pet visits can be arranged for feeding, fresh water, puppy check-ins, garden breaks, cat care, small pet care and companionship.
