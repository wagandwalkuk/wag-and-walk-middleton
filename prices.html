Simple Prices

How much does a dog walker cost?

Every walk is one-to-one, with the pace and route shaped around your dog. The table shows the price for each service and the lower rate for an agreed schedule of at least three visits a week.

A single walk can give your dog a welcome break in the day. If it suits their routine better to have care spread out, split-day options provide two separate visits instead of one longer gap between outings. Choose two 30-minute walks, or a 30-minute walk and a 15-minute drop-in. A 60-minute solo walk is also available for dogs who benefit from more time outdoors. Longer walks and split-day bookings are by arrangement, subject to location and availability.

House visits can cover feeding, company and a comfort break. Dogs from the same household can be cared for together where suitable, with an additional £5 per visit.

Based in Middleton, I also cover Rochdale and nearby areas where the diary allows. For a changing rota, see Flex Credits. You can also read more about solo dog walking.

Booking Terms

Standard Booking Policy

Standard walks are booked for a specific date and time.

Because this slot is reserved for your dog, changes and cancellations are handled as follows:

  • More than 48 hours’ notice: no charge
  • 24–48 hours’ notice: 50% of the walk fee applies
  • Less than 24 hours’ notice: the full walk fee applies
  • Rearrangements are treated as cancellations and new bookings

This helps protect reserved diary time, especially when the slot cannot be offered to another client at short notice.
