About The Series

Real decisions, systems and lessons

Each Behind The Lead article looks at a genuine business problem, decision, process improvement or lesson learned while building Wag & Walk Middleton. The aim is not to present a polished success story. It is to document the practical work behind creating a dependable local service.

That means sharing what worked, what did not, and how each new customer or operational constraint shaped the next version of the business.
