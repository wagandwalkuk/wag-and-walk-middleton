Booking Terms

Standard Booking Policy

Standard walks are booked for a specific date and time window.

Because this slot is reserved for your dog, changes and cancellations are handled as follows:

  • More than 24 hours’ notice: no charge
  • Less than 24 hours’ notice: 50% of the walk fee applies
  • Same-day cancellation: the full walk fee applies

This helps protect reserved diary time, especially when the slot cannot be offered to another client at short notice.

Service Questions

Dog walking services: common questions

What dog walking services do you offer?

We offer 30-minute and 60-minute solo dog walks, plus house visits for dogs who need a comfort break, feeding, company or basic care at home.

Do you offer a regular dog walking service?

Yes. Recurring bookings are available for clients with a settled weekly routine, subject to diary availability. For changing schedules, Flex Credits can provide more flexibility.

Are your dog walks one to one?

Yes. Walks are solo as standard, with no mixing of dogs from different households. Dogs from the same household can be walked together where the arrangement suits them.

Where are your dog walking services available?

We cover Middleton and nearby communities including Rhodes, Alkrington, Bowlee, Langley, Simister, Blackley, Chadderton, Mills Hill, Hopwood Hall, Firwood Park, Westwood, Rochdale, Manchester and M24. Check coverage for your area.
