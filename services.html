Booking Terms

Standard Booking Policy

Standard walks are booked for a specific date and time window.

Because this slot is reserved for your dog, changes and cancellations are handled as follows:

  • More than 24 hours’ notice: no charge
  • Less than 24 hours’ notice: 50% of the walk fee applies
  • Same-day cancellation: the full walk fee applies

This helps protect reserved diary time, especially when the slot cannot be offered to another client at short notice.
