Booking Terms

Standard Booking Policy

Standard walks are booked for a specific date and time.

Because this slot is reserved for your dog, changes and cancellations are handled as follows:

  • More than 48 hours’ notice: no charge
  • 24–48 hours’ notice: 50% of the walk fee applies
  • Less than 24 hours’ notice: the full walk fee applies
  • Rearrangements are treated as cancellations and new bookings

This helps protect reserved diary time, especially when the slot cannot be offered to another client at short notice.

Need more flexibility?

Flex Credits include rearrangements subject to availability, making them better suited to changing work patterns.
