Dog-First Care

Why Choose Solo Dog Walks?

Solo walks give your dog one-to-one attention without being placed into a large mixed group. The route, pace and amount of sniffing can be adjusted around the individual dog rather than a group schedule.

  • Focused attention from the walker
  • No mixed-group walks
  • Routes tailored to personality, pace and needs
  • Calmer support for nervous dogs
  • Measured outings for puppies building confidence
  • Comfortable pacing for older dogs

Dogs from the same household can be walked together when that arrangement suits them.

Changing Schedules

Flexible Dog Walking For Busy Owners

Fixed weekly bookings suit many owners, but shift work, office changes and unexpected diary changes can make dog care harder to plan.

Flex Credits allow eligible walks to be booked and rearranged subject to availability. They are designed for shift workers and owners whose required days change from week to week.

For practical planning ideas, read our guide to dog walking for shift workers.

Flex Credits can help with:

  • Changing rotas
  • Occasional office days
  • Weeks that do not follow a fixed pattern
  • Booking walks when support is needed

Local Dog Walking Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find a dog walker near me?

Start by checking whether the walker covers your postcode, then ask about insurance, DBS status, walking style, availability and how your dog will be introduced. Wag & Walk Middleton offers a free meet and greet before the first walk.

Do you offer group walks?

No. Wag & Walk Middleton provides solo walks as standard and does not mix dogs from different client households. Dogs from the same household can be walked together.

Which areas do you cover?

Regular coverage includes Middleton, M24, Rhodes, Bowlee, Alkrington, Langley, Simister, Mills Hill, Blackley, Chadderton, Hopwood Hall, Firwood Park, Westwood, Rochdale and selected nearby Manchester areas.

Can I book a meet and greet first?

Yes. Every new client can arrange a free meet and greet to discuss their dog, routine, access and the support they need before walks begin.

Do you walk nervous dogs?

Solo walks can suit many nervous dogs because routes, pace and handling can be tailored to the individual. Suitability is discussed honestly during the meet and greet.
