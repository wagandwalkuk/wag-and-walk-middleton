Credit Terms

Flex Credits Policy

Flex Credits are designed for owners whose schedules can change.

  • All Flex Credits are valid for 8 weeks from purchase
  • Credits can be combined for longer walks
  • Credits cannot be split into smaller bookings
  • Walks can be rearranged subject to availability
  • Same-day cancellations use the credit
  • Unused credits expire after 8 weeks

Flex Credits are subject to the Wag & Walk Middleton Flex Credits Terms, including the 8-week validity period and same-day cancellation policy.

Examples

How Credits Can Be Used

Simple uses

  • Flex 30 = 30-minute walk
  • Flex 60 = 60-minute walk
  • Flex 30 can also be used for one house visit

Combined credits

  • Flex 30 + Flex 30 = 60-minute walk
  • Flex 60 + Flex 30 = 90-minute walk
  • Flex 60 + Flex 60 = 120-minute walk
  • One Flex 60 cannot be split into two separate 30-minute walks

A Flexible Alternative

Booked a walk for Thursday but your plans suddenly change?

With Flex Credits, you can move the booking to another available day without paying additional rearrangement fees, subject to availability. If the walk is cancelled on the same day, the credit is still used.
