Our Approach

Personal care, calm handling and local knowledge

Wag & Walk Middleton was created for owners who want a reliable dog walker who treats each pet as an individual. The service is intentionally personal: solo walks, careful introductions, sensible routes and regular updates after visits.

Being local to Middleton matters. It means knowing which roads get busy, which parks suit different dogs, where calmer routes can be found, and when a dog would benefit from a simple neighbourhood stroll instead of a busier outing.

Every booking starts with understanding your routine, your dog's personality and any health, handling or home-care notes that keep things safe.
