Home Care

Practical, reassuring visits for pets at home

House visits are ideal for cats, puppies, older dogs, small pets and dogs who do not need a full walk but still need company and care. Visits can include feeding, water changes, toilet breaks, medication prompts where agreed, litter tray checks and simple home checks.

Each visit is tailored to your pet's routine. You receive a clear update afterwards so you know your pet has been seen, settled and cared for.
